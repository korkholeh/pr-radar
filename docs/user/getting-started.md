# Getting started

PR Radar is for team leads and managers. Ask your admin to create your account — they do it with `manage.py
createsuperuser` or through `/admin/` — and they will give you a username and password. If you are installing
PR Radar yourself, start from the [user guide index](README.md).

## Log in

Open the app's URL. Every page requires an account, so you land on **Log in**. Enter your **Username** and
**Password** and select **Log in**. If you forgot your password, use the password reset flow linked from the
login screen.

After logging in you land on **Overview**, the page linked from the **PR Radar** title in the top bar — the
global dashboard, with KPI cards, charts and tables for every project and repository you have access to. See
`docs/user/dashboards.md` for how to read it.

## Switch theme

Every page (including the login screen) has a **Theme** dropdown with three options: **System**, **Light**, and
**Dark**. **System** follows your OS/browser setting. Once you are logged in, your choice is saved to your
account and applied on any device you log in from; before you log in, it is remembered only in this browser.

## Switch language

Next to the theme dropdown is a **Language** dropdown: **English** or **Українська**. Once you are logged in,
your choice is saved to your account and follows you to a new device or a new browser session. Before you log
in, only this browser remembers it.

## Log out

Select **Log out** in the top bar.
