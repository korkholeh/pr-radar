## Definitions

### `ai_active_people`

Distinct people who authored at least one AI-cohort pull request in the period (not the sum of each day's distinct count).

### `ai_pr_count`

Pull requests created on a day that belong to the AI cohort.

### `ai_pr_share`

Share of pull requests merged on a day that belong to the AI cohort.

### `ai_status_breakdown`

Pull requests created in the period, grouped by their detected AI status.

### `ai_tool_breakdown`

AI-cohort pull requests created in the period, grouped by disclosed tool. A PR with no disclosed tool contributes to no label.

### `disclosure_mismatch_count`

Pull requests created on a day with an open disclosure-mismatch violation — same population as the policy console's disclosure mismatch list.

### `disclosure_rate`

Share of AI-cohort pull requests created on a day whose AI disclosure is partial or substantial (as opposed to missing or ambiguous).

### `violations_by_rule`

Violations created in the period, grouped by rule code.

### `violations_new`

Violations created on a day, regardless of their current status.

### `violations_open`

Violations created on or before the end of the period that are still open — a current snapshot bounded by created_at, matching the policy console.

### `cycle_time_p50`

From first commit to merge, for PRs merged in the period.

### `lead_time_p50`

From ready for review to merge, for PRs merged in the period.

### `lead_time_p90`

From ready for review to merge, for PRs merged in the period (90th percentile).

### `open_prs`

Pull requests open at the end of a day, reconstructed from their dates.

### `pr_size_buckets`

PRs merged in the period, grouped by size bucket (XS/S/M/L/XL, boundaries from PR_SIZE_BUCKETS).

### `pr_size_p50`

Median effective lines changed (additions + deletions) for PRs merged in the period.

### `prs_closed_unmerged`

Pull requests closed on a day without being merged.

### `prs_excluded_from_metrics`

Pull requests created on a day by a bot or a person excluded from metrics — the population every other metric leaves out, surfaced rather than silently dropped.

### `prs_merged`

Pull requests merged on a day — the flow throughput counter.

### `prs_opened`

Pull requests created on a day.

### `review_load_share`

Share of reviews given in the period that fall on the top REVIEW_LOAD_TOP_N reviewers by review count — a bottleneck-risk indicator.

### `reviewer_response_p50`

From the PR becoming ready for review (falling back to its creation) to this reviewer's review, for reviews given in the period — an approximation: no review-request timestamp is stored, so this is not measured from the actual request.

### `reviews_given`

Reviews submitted on a day.

### `stale_prs`

Open, non-draft pull requests with no activity for more than STALE_DAYS, as of the end of a day.

### `time_to_first_review_p50`

From ready for review to the first review, for PRs merged in the period.

### `time_to_first_review_p90`

From ready for review to the first review, for PRs merged in the period (90th percentile).

### `waiting_review_24h`

Pull requests ready for review with no review yet, waiting for more than WAITING_REVIEW_HOURS, as of the end of a day.

### `wip_per_person`

Open, non-draft pull requests divided by the number of distinct authors holding one, as of the end of a day.

### `churn_21d`

Median share of a merged PR's lines that were themselves changed again within 21 days, for PRs merged in the period. Empty until manage.py compute_churn has run.

### `ci_first_pass_rate`

Share of PRs merged on a day whose first CI-covered commit passed (SUCCESS). PRs with no first-CI-commit record are excluded from both sides.

### `followup_fix_rate`

(heuristic) Share of merged PRs followed within FOLLOWUP_FIX_WINDOW_DAYS by a hotfix/fix PR overlapping at least FOLLOWUP_FIX_FILE_OVERLAP of their files.

Params: file_overlap=0.5, heuristic=True, window_days=14

### `revert_rate`

Share of PRs merged on a day that were later reverted.

### `review_comments_per_100_lines`

Review comments per 100 effective lines changed, for PRs merged on a day.

### `review_rounds_avg`

Average number of review rounds (CHANGES_REQUESTED + 1) for PRs merged on a day.

### `rework_rate`

Share of PRs merged on a day that had at least one commit after their first review.

### `rubber_stamp_rate`

Share of PRs merged on a day that were approved suspiciously fast with no comment on a large change (RUBBER_STAMP_MAX_MINUTES).

### `self_merge_rate`

Share of PRs merged on a day that were merged by their own author.

### `test_change_ratio`

Share of PRs merged on a day that changed test code.

### `test_lines_ratio`

Test-file lines changed divided by all effective lines changed, for PRs merged on a day.

### `ai_only_approval_rate`

Share of pull requests merged on a day, among those that were approved at all, whose approvals came only from bot accounts — no human other than the author approved.

### `ai_review_coverage`

Share of pull requests merged on a day that one of the AI reviewers named in the AI policy reviewed. Empty until at least one AI reviewer login is configured.

### `high_risk_ai_pr_rate`

Share of AI-cohort pull requests merged on a day that touched a path an active sensitive-path rule classifies as high risk, advisory rules included. Always measured over the AI cohort, whatever cohort the page is filtered to.

### `quality_gate_bypass_rate`

Share of pull requests merged on a day whose last observed status-check rollup was a failure or an error. A repository that runs no checks cannot bypass them, so PRs with no check at all are excluded from both sides.

### `structural_signal_rate`

Share of pull requests merged on a day carrying at least one structural signal — a heuristic about the shape of the change, never a quoted AI marker.
